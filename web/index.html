Why FŁÝ // protection.stack

Every layer below is implemented, not aspirational. Each one adds cost for whoever tries to pull your source back out.

01 · Compile

Source is lowered through Luau, not string-rewritten and hoped for.

02 · Translate

Official bytecode is remapped onto a private instruction set.

03 · Virtualize

A generated VM executes the remapped stream. Opcodes shuffle per build.

04 · Encrypt

The proto blob is encrypted before it ever leaves the server.
